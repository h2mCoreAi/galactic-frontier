## ⚡ The Ultimate Space Combat Experience

**Galactic Frontier** thrusts you into the depths of an infinite 2D universe where danger lurks behind every asteroid. Master physics-based ship control, unleash devastating weapons, and survive waves of relentless space pirates as you fight for your place among the stars.

### 🎯 What Makes This Game Unique?

✨ **Physics-Based Combat** - Experience realistic momentum, thrust, and braking systems that reward skillful piloting

💥 **Dynamic Power-Up System** - Collect health restores and unlock the devastating **Fan-Shot** ability that unleashes a spread of 15 projectiles in all directions

🔥 **Afterburner System** - Limited-energy boost that adds strategic depth to combat and escape scenarios

🎖️ **Progressive Difficulty** - Each level scales enemy speed, damage, and spawn rates - the challenge never stops growing

🏆 **Competitive Leaderboards** - Compete globally with Discord authentication, track your accuracy, and analyze your session statistics

⚔️ **Three Enemy Classes** - Face off against nimble **Hornets**, balanced **Marauders**, and heavily-armed **Dreadnoughts**

## 🎮 Gameplay Features

### Ship Control System
- **Mouse Movement** - Intuitive ship rotation for precise aiming
- **W Key** - Thrust forward through the void
- **S Key** - Activate reverse thrusters (visual feedback with cyan glow)
- **Spacebar** - Engage afterburner for double-speed bursts (limited energy)
- **Left Click** - Fire projectiles at your enemies

### Combat Mechanics
- **Health System** - 100 HP with visual health bar - survive the chaos
- **Projectile Combat** - Fast-moving projectiles with cooldown management
- **Enemy AI** - Intelligent enemies that pursue, shoot, and collide with strategic timing
- **Collision Damage** - Both ship and enemies take damage from direct impacts

### Power-Up System
- **Health Power-Ups** - Restore 10 HP to extend your survival runs
- **Fan-Shot Power-Up** - Transform your single-shot into a 15-projectile spread pattern for 10 seconds
- **Visual Effects** - Stunning particle effects and orbs accompany power-up activations

### Scoring & Progression
- **Combo System** - Chain kills to build score multipliers (up to multiple X multipliers)
- **Level System** - Progress through infinite levels with increasing difficulty
- **Enemy Values** - Hornets (10 pts), Marauders (20 pts), Dreadnoughts (50 pts)
- **Score Multipliers** - Time your kills correctly to maximize your score

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Start** - Press Space or Enter at the main menu
2. **Authenticate** (Optional) - Login with Discord to save scores online
3. **Pilot Your Ship**:
   - Move mouse to rotate
   - Hold W to thrust
   - Hold S to brake (see the cyan glow!)
   - Hold Spacebar for afterburner boost
   - Left click to shoot
4. **Survive & Score**:
   - Destroy enemies for points
   - Chain kills for combo multipliers
   - Collect power-ups to extend your run
   - Level up for bigger challenges
5. **Compete** - Check the leaderboards to see where you rank!

## 🔥 Tips for Survival

💡 **Master the Brake** - Use reverse thrusters (S key) to slow down and dodge incoming projectiles. Watch for the cyan glow!

💡 **Manage Afterburner** - Save your afterburner energy for escapes. It regenerates slowly, so use it wisely.

💡 **Prioritize Targets** - Focus Dreadnoughts first when possible - they're slow but incredibly dangerous.

💡 **Combo Building** - Kill enemies quickly in succession to build your score multiplier. Timing matters!

💡 **Health Management** - Keep an eye on your health bar. Green = safe, Yellow = caution, Red = critical!

💡 **Power-Up Timing** - Fan-Shot is incredibly powerful against groups. Save it for when enemies cluster.
